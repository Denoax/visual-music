PROJECTED LANDSCAPES — original geometry and adaptation record

The seven geometry-only GLBs were supplied by Mani in
BUMER_EXE_PROJECTED_LANDSCAPES_CONTINUATION_c9b7180.zip. Their explicit
ridge profiles and folded fin are reproducibly authored by the included
standard-library Python script, preserved unchanged under
sources/projected-landscapes-handoff/code/bake_landscapes.py. No external
artist model, texture, reference footage or model-generated visual media
is used. Hashes and original provenance are in assets/manifest.json and the
supplied ASSET_INDEX.json. No new third-party licence or CC0 grant is inferred.

Original runtime Three.js/GLSL adaptation: bounded world anchors, selective
contours, travelling washes, positive uniform scale/Y rotation, terrain depth,
LOD blending, footprint lighting gates and near-camera visibility fades.

Research only; no publication grants asset reuse:
Studio Lemercier / Joanie Lemercier — Fuji (2013), La Montagne (2016–2018),
No-logram (2017). Fuji credits music Paul Jebanasam and concept/story/scenography
Juliette Bibasse. Universal Everything, Radiohead, Nigel Godrich and Stanley
Donwood — Polyfauna (2014). HelloEnjoy — Lights and Fantasynth.
Official pages and inspection limits: sources/projected-landscapes-record.md.
Research photographs remain separate from runtime and are included only as
attributed private review evidence. Existing audio/font/UI/Three.js notices apply.

CURRENT RETROWAVE REPLACEMENT
The six older mountain runtime meshes are retired and recoverable. Four new
retro_range/retro_saddle near/far GLBs are original supplied deterministic geometry
from BUMER_EXE_RETROWAVE_BEAUTY_CONTINUATION_3bddfb2/code/bake_retrowave.py.
The folded_light_fin is retained. Active assets: assets/landscapes.json.
